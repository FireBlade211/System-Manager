Wording and Typography

This page shows the guidelines for how text in your plugin should be worded and the style of typography you should use for your plugin pages.

Wording

Use simple, concise, and short messages. Make sure the text is simple and easy to understand for most users. Do not use complicated words or technical language that users may not understand. Use punctuation wherever possible, and make sure text is properly capitalized.

EXCEPTION: If your plugin is mostly intended for power users or other users who may understand technical terminology, you may use technical terminology in your plugin.

Typography

Keep your text in a Windows XP, 2003-like legacy style. Most text should use the Tahoma or Verdana font. The recommended styles are:
